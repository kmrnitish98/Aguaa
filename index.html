What is a Marriage Biodata? (विवाह बायोडाटा क्या है?)

A marriage biodata (also known as a shaadi biodata or matrimonial CV) is a concise, formal document summarizing an individual's personal, educational, professional, and family background for arranged marriage proposals. Unlike a standard employment resume, a wedding biodata places deep cultural emphasis on family values, religious lineage, gotra, ancestral roots, and astrological harmony.

In Indian tradition—particularly across Bihar, Uttar Pradesh, and Jharkhand—exchanging biodata is the very first sacred step in establishing an alliance. A clean, respectful, and aesthetically pleasing biodata conveys sincerity, pride in family heritage, and transparency, ensuring that both families can make an informed, dignified decision.
